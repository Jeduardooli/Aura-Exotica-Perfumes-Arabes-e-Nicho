OUR STORY

Founded in 1974, Swiss Arabian has been at the forefront of the fragrance industry for over five decades. Our journey began with a vision to blend the rich traditions of Arabian perfumery with Swiss precision and quality.

Today, we continue to create exquisite fragrances that capture the essence of the Orient, using only the finest ingredients from around the world.
